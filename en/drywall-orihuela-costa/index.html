We work all along Orihuela Costa: Playa Flamenca, La Zenia, Cabo Roig, Campoamor, Villamartín, Las Ramblas and Lomas de Cabo Roig. We speak English and German and can manage the job for owners who live abroad.

What we do in Orihuela Costa

Orihuela Costa is mostly apartments, bungalows and villas, many of them holiday homes and rentals. Typical jobs: soundproofing a shared wall or ceiling, turning a closed terrace or garage into a bedroom, splitting a living room, and modern false ceilings with LED lighting.

  • Soundproofing walls: an independent acoustic lining in front of your wall, with mineral wool and double board. No need to touch your neighbour's side. From €52/m² + VAT.
  • Soundproofing ceilings: for footsteps and impact noise from the flat above, with a suspended acoustic ceiling.
  • Partition walls: split a living room to add a bedroom or an office, with a door opening and insulation inside. From €44/m² + VAT.
  • False ceilings and coving with LED: hide cables and pipes, add spotlights or indirect lighting. From €32/m² + VAT.
  • Moisture-resistant board (green, type H1) for bathrooms and kitchens near the sea.
  • Built-in units: TV walls, wardrobes and niches in drywall, ready to paint.
