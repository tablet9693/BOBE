Drywall Prices on the Costa Blanca (2026): What Things Cost

BOBE Construcciones · 17 November 2026

In Spain drywall is usually called pladur (after the best-known brand). These are our guide prices for 2026 in the Vega Baja, Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa, Elche, Alicante and Murcia, with materials, labour and clean-up included.

Guide prices per m² (excluding VAT)

  • Partition wall (one board each side, mineral wool inside): from €44/m².
  • Continuous false ceiling: from €32/m².
  • Insulated wall lining for cold or damp walls: from €30/m².
  • Soundproofing (independent lining, double board, acoustic strip): from €52/m².
  • Suspended tile ceiling (60×60, for offices and shops): from €28/m².

Minimum job: €350. VAT on home renovations for private owners is usually 10%; otherwise 21%.

What makes the price go up or down

  • Height of the wall or ceiling and access (stairs, scaffolding).
  • Openings: doors, windows, coving for LED lights.
  • Special boards: moisture-resistant (bathrooms, kitchens), fire-resistant, acoustic.
  • Sockets and lights to move or add.
  • Finish: ready to paint, or painted.

Example: adding a bedroom

Splitting a living room with a partition of up to 10 m², with a door opening, mineral wool and joints ready to paint, starts at around €590 + VAT, built in about two days.

Frequently asked questions

Is pladur the same as drywall?

Yes. Pladur is a brand name that is used in Spain for plasterboard / drywall in general.

Do your prices include VAT?

No. Guide prices exclude VAT; the written quote shows the total with VAT.

Is there a call-out fee?

No. The visit, measuring and quote are free.
