How to Soundproof an Apartment in Spain (Costa Blanca guide)

BOBE Construcciones · 17 November 2026

Many apartments on the Costa Blanca were built quickly, with thin brick partitions between flats. If you can hear your neighbour's TV, conversations or footsteps, the good news is that it can usually be fixed from inside your own apartment, without touching your neighbour's side and without heavy building work.

First: what kind of noise is it?

  • Airborne noise (voices, TV, music, barking) travels through the air and the wall. The fix is an acoustic lining on the wall.
  • Impact noise (footsteps, chairs, things dropped upstairs) travels through the structure. The fix is usually an acoustic ceiling.
  • Vibrations from a machine (air conditioning units, pumps) are a different problem and are often better solved at the source.

Option 1: acoustic wall lining

We build a new, independent drywall wall a few centimetres in front of the existing one. It has its own metal frame (not screwed into the old wall), mineral wool inside and two layers of plasterboard, with an acoustic strip around the edges so vibrations don't pass through. You lose roughly 8–10 cm of room depth.

It noticeably reduces airborne noise such as voices and TV. It reduces impact noise much less, so for footsteps from upstairs the ceiling is the right place to act.

Option 2: acoustic ceiling

For footsteps and knocks from the flat above, we hang a suspended drywall ceiling on acoustic hangers, with mineral wool above it and double board. The room loses some height (usually 10–15 cm), and lights are reinstalled in the new ceiling.

How much does it cost?

As a guide, our soundproofing starts at €52/m² excluding VAT for a wall lining, materials and labour included, with a minimum job of €350. A typical bedroom wall of 3.5 × 2.6 m is around 9 m². The final price depends on height, sockets and switches to move, skirting boards and finish, and we confirm it in writing after measuring.

How long does it take?

A single wall is usually built in 1–2 days, plus 1–2 more days for joints ready to paint. It is a dry build: no bricks, no cement, very little dust, and we clean up every day.

Do I need permission?

If the work is only inside your apartment, you usually don't need the community's permission. Some town halls ask for a simple minor works notice (declaración responsable de obra menor); check with your town hall or ask us and we'll tell you what is usual in your area.

If you live abroad

Many of our clients on the coast are not in Spain all year. We can visit with your key holder or property manager, send you the written quote and progress photos by WhatsApp or email, and finish while you're away.

Frequently asked questions

Will soundproofing remove all the noise?

It reduces it a lot, especially voices and TV, but no system makes a flat completely silent. We explain realistic results on the free visit.

How much room do I lose?

About 8–10 cm on a soundproofed wall and 10–15 cm of height on an acoustic ceiling.
